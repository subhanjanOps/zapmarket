In India, the **Goods and Services Tax (GST)** is a unified indirect tax applied at multiple rates depending on the product category. On ZapMarket, every price you see is GST-inclusive — there are no surprise taxes at checkout.

## GST rates by category

| Category | GST Rate |
|---|---|
| Essential goods (food, books) | 0–5% |
| Electronics | 12–18% |
| Luxury goods | 28% |

## What this means for buyers

When a product is listed at ₹1,180, the base price is ₹1,000 and ₹180 is GST at 18%. You pay exactly what's displayed — no additions at checkout.

## Why transparency matters

Some platforms show base prices and add GST at checkout, making final prices feel higher than expected. ZapMarket's GST-inclusive approach means you always know your true cost upfront.
