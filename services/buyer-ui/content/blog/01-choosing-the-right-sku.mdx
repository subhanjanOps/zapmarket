A **SKU** (Stock Keeping Unit) is a unique identifier for a specific variant of a product. When you see a product listing with multiple options — different sizes, colors, or configurations — each combination has its own SKU.

## Why SKUs matter for buyers

When you add an item to your cart, the SKU ensures you get exactly what you selected. A red T-shirt in size M has a different SKU than the same shirt in size L or blue.

## How to read a SKU

Many sellers use structured SKU codes like `TSHIRT-RED-M` or `LAPTOP-16GB-512SSD`. The segments typically encode the product family, variant attributes, and sometimes the seller's internal reference.

## Tips for choosing the right variant

1. **Check the attribute selectors carefully** — color names can differ between sellers
2. **Read the SKU description** — it often contains exact specs
3. **Verify stock status** — some SKUs may show as unavailable
4. **Compare prices per variant** — prices can vary by configuration

Getting the SKU right means fewer returns and a smoother shopping experience.
