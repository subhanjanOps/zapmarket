ZapMarket verifies every seller before they can list products. This process protects buyers from fraud and ensures accountability throughout the marketplace.

## What verification covers

**Identity verification** — sellers must provide government-issued ID and a valid business address.

**GST registration** — sellers must have a valid GSTIN to sell taxable goods.

**Bank account verification** — payouts go only to verified bank accounts to prevent fraud.

**Product authenticity** — high-value categories like electronics require brand authorization letters.

## What the verified badge means

When you see a **Verified Seller** badge on a listing, it means ZapMarket has completed all checks. This does not guarantee product quality, but it ensures the seller is a legitimate, accountable business.

## What to do if something goes wrong

Even with verified sellers, issues can arise. Use the **Report Seller** option on any listing to flag concerns. ZapMarket's trust team reviews all reports within 24 hours.

Your safety is our priority.
