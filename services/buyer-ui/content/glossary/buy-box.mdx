The **buy box** is the primary purchase option shown prominently on a product page. When multiple sellers list the same product, ZapMarket's algorithm selects the best offer (considering price, seller rating, and shipping speed) to feature in the buy box. Other offers are still accessible but secondary. Winning the buy box significantly increases a seller's sales.
