A **seller rating** is an aggregate score (typically 1–5 stars) calculated from buyer reviews of a seller's service, product quality, and shipping speed. On ZapMarket, seller ratings are displayed on every listing. Sellers with higher ratings are more likely to win the buy box and appear higher in search results.
