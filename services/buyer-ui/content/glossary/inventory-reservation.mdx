**Inventory reservation** is the process of temporarily holding stock for a buyer when they initiate checkout. This prevents overselling — if two buyers try to purchase the last unit simultaneously, only one succeeds. On ZapMarket, reservations are held for a few minutes; if checkout is not completed, the stock is released back to available inventory.
