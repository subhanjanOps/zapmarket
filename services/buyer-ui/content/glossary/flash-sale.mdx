A **flash sale** is a limited-time promotional event where products are offered at steep discounts for a short window (often a few hours). Stock is limited and sells out fast. ZapMarket's deals pages feature active flash sales with countdown timers so you know exactly how long remains. Inventory is reserved on a first-come-first-served basis.
