**Goods and Services Tax (GST)** is India's unified indirect tax system. On ZapMarket, all displayed prices are GST-inclusive. Rates range from 0% for essential goods to 28% for luxury items. As a buyer, you never pay more than the listed price — no surprise taxes are added at checkout.
