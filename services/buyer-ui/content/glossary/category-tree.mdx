A **category tree** is the hierarchical structure used to organize products on a marketplace. On ZapMarket, the top-level categories (e.g., Electronics, Clothing, Home & Kitchen) branch into subcategories (e.g., Laptops, T-Shirts, Cookware). Each product is assigned to a leaf node in this tree, making it discoverable through browsing and filtering. The category tree also determines applicable GST rates and return policies.
